Backer Rod

Use backer rod for joints deeper than 1/2" to control depth and save material.

Joint Design

Ideal depth-to-width ratio is 1:2 for maximum movement capability. A 1/4" wide joint wants about 1/8" of depth.

Temperature

Apply between 40°F-100°F for optimal adhesion and cure performance.

How to Measure Your Joint

Length

Measure the total linear feet of every joint you're sealing. Add each run together. Five 20-ft windows is 100 linear feet, not 20.

Width

Measure the gap across, at its widest point. If the gap changes size along the run, use the widest measurement so you do not come up short.

Depth

Measure how deep the sealant will sit, not how deep the hole is. With backer rod in place, depth is the distance from the rod to the surface.
